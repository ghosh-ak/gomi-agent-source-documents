General Collection Rules

  • Put waste out by 8:30 a.m. on the designated collection day.
  • Use the required Kitami City designated bag or disposal ticket.
  • Incorrectly separated waste or waste placed without the required designated bag may be collected and opened for inspection.
  • Collection may be suspended because of weather or road conditions. Check Kitami City's website or official waste-sorting app for current notices.
  • Waste from businesses and waste removed by contractors may follow different rules and must not be treated automatically as household waste.

Waste Categories

Burnable (燃やすごみ)

  • Bag: Kitami City designated orange bag (橙色)
  • Fee: Paid
  • Normal frequency: Twice per week
  • Core rule: Burnable material with a maximum dimension of 30 cm or less, unless a specific item rule states otherwise.
  • Common examples: Food waste, paper scraps, small wooden products, clothing and cloth cut to the required size, leather goods, rubber goods, and hygienic plastic items.
  • Preparation: Drain food waste thoroughly. Cut clothing and large cloth to 1 m square or less and write 切っています on the bag when required. Remove detachable metal parts and dispose of them according to their own category. Apply specific safety rules to sharp or hazardous items.

Unburnable (燃やさないごみ)

  • Bag: Kitami City designated young-grass green bag (若草色)
  • Fee: Paid
  • Normal frequency: Twice per month
  • Core rule: Non-burnable household items and burnable items larger than 30 cm, generally up to 1 m and 10 kg, unless a specific item rule states otherwise.
  • Common examples: Ceramics, glassware, metal products, hard plastic products, small household appliances, mats, umbrellas, and mixed-material products.
  • Preparation: Wrap broken glass, knives, scissors, and other sharp objects in paper and write 危険 on the designated bag. Remove batteries from electrical products. Items longer than 1 m or heavier than 10 kg are generally large-sized waste.

Recyclable (資源ごみ)

  • Bag or bundling: Transparent or translucent bag, or tied bundles for paper depending on material
  • Fee: Free
  • Normal frequency: Once per week
  • Keep cans, bottles, PET bottles, and different paper types separate.
  • Separate paper by type and tie it with string. Rinse and dry cans, bottles, and PET bottles. Do not crush cans or PET bottles.
  • Remove PET-bottle caps and labels and place them with plastic containers and packaging. Metal bottle caps are unburnable waste.
  • Cosmetic, medicine, and oil bottles are unburnable waste. Apply dictionary exceptions to dirty or uncleanable items.

Plastic Containers and Packaging (プラスチック製容器包装)

  • Bag: Transparent or translucent bag
  • Fee: Free
  • Normal frequency: Once per week
  • Eligibility: Plastic containers or packaging that held or wrapped a product and became unnecessary after use, typically marked with the プラ symbol.
  • Empty, wash, and dry items. Do not place smaller bags inside the main collection bag. Break eligible polystyrene foam into pieces of about 15 cm.
  • Food-contaminated items that cannot be cleaned are burnable waste. Plastic products that are not containers or packaging generally do not belong to this category.

Diapers (紙おむつ類)

  • Bag: Transparent or translucent bag, maximum 45 L
  • Fee: Free
  • Normal frequency: Twice per week
  • Maximum weight: 10 kg per bag
  • Eligible items include disposable diapers, cloth diapers, disposable pants, urine pads, and baby wipes. Remove solid waste first.
  • Waterproof sheets, pet diapers, and toilet sheets are burnable waste. Eligible diaper waste may alternatively use the paid orange burnable-waste bag.

Hazardous (有害ごみ)

  • Bag: Transparent or translucent bag
  • Fee: Free
  • Collection timing: On applicable burnable or unburnable collection days
  • Separate each type. Examples include batteries, rechargeable batteries, spray cans, gas cylinders, lighters, fluorescent tubes, bulbs, mercury products, and eligible containers for volatile or flammable liquids.
  • Use up spray-can and gas-cylinder contents and do not puncture containers. Protect fluorescent tubes and bulbs from breakage. Remove batteries from appliances whenever possible.

Large-sized Waste (粗大ごみ)

  • Fee: Paid
  • Method: Advance application required; do not use the neighborhood waste station.
  • General threshold: Usually more than 1 m or more than 10 kg, plus specifically listed items.
  • Contact the Large-sized Waste Reception Center, confirm the collection arrangements and tickets, and place the item at the agreed location by 8:30 a.m.
  • Telephone: 0157-22-0053
  • Hours: Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 5:00 p.m., excluding public holidays and December 29 through January 3.

Waste Not Accepted by the City (市が処理できないごみ)

Not accepted at neighborhood waste stations or through normal city collection. Examples include major home appliances, computers and monitors, vehicle tires, lead-acid batteries, fuels, waste oil, propane cylinders, toxic pesticides, paint, thinner, pianos, fire extinguishers, needles, and engine-powered equipment. Contact the applicable retailer, manufacturer, recycling route, medical institution, or specialist identified in the item dictionary.

Small Appliance Drop-off (小型家電（無料）)

Free collection at designated drop-off locations, not neighborhood waste stations. The general drop-box opening is approximately 30 cm by 40 cm. Remove batteries before drop-off. Personal computers and appliances covered by the Home Appliance Recycling Law are excluded.

Textile Drop-off (繊維資源（無料）)

Free collection at designated drop-off locations. Eligible items include clean, dry, reusable clothing, towels, blankets without filling, and sheets. Wet, dirty, odorous, stuffed, very small, carpet-like, or mat-like textiles are excluded.

Prototype Decision Rules

  1. Identify the exact object and its function, not only its material.
  2. Check the item dictionary for an exact or close match.
  3. Check condition, contamination, sharpness, liquid contents, and batteries.
  4. Check recycling marks such as PET and プラ.
  5. Check relevant thresholds, including 15 cm, 30 cm, 1 m, and 10 kg.
  6. Confirm whether the item is household waste, business waste, or contractor-removed waste.
  7. Apply the exact dictionary instruction before the general category rule.
  8. Ask one focused follow-up question when required information is missing.
  9. If no official rule supports the answer, return an uncertain result and refer the user to Kitami City's Waste Management Division.
